IT STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE: PASSWORD RESET

Step 1: Go to https://password.company.internal
Step 2: Click 'Forgot Password'
Step 3: Enter your employee ID or email
Step 4: Choose reset method: SMS OTP or Email OTP
Step 5: Enter the 6-digit OTP received
Step 6: Set a new password meeting the policy:
  - Minimum 12 characters
  - At least 1 uppercase letter
  - At least 1 number
  - At least 1 special character
  - Cannot reuse last 5 passwords

The new password syncs to Active Directory within 5 minutes.
If you do not receive the OTP within 2 minutes, contact the IT desk at ext. 4500.
