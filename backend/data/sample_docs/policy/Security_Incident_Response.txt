SECURITY INCIDENT RESPONSE PROCEDURE

Reporting a phishing email:
1. Do NOT click any links in the email
2. Use the 'Report Phishing' button in Outlook (toolbar)
   OR forward to phishing@company.internal
3. Delete the email from your inbox
4. If you already clicked a link or entered credentials:
   a. Immediately reset your password (see IT_SOP_PasswordReset.pdf)
   b. Call the IT Security desk at ext. 4700
   c. Disconnect from the corporate network until cleared

Response SLAs:
- SOC acknowledgement: within 1 business hour
- Initial assessment: within 4 business hours
- Containment: within 24 hours for Severity 1 incidents

Other incident types (lost device, unauthorized access, data leak):
- Report immediately to security@company.internal
- Do not attempt to investigate or remediate on your own
