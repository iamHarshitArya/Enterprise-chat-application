ONBOARDING CHECKLIST FOR NEW HIRES

Required documents from new hire:
1. Signed offer letter (counter-sign and return)
2. PAN card copy
3. Aadhaar card copy
4. Educational certificates (10th, 12th, degree)
5. Previous employment relieving letter
6. Bank account details (cancelled cheque or passbook copy)
7. Two passport-size photographs
8. Form 12B (if joining with previous PF balance)

Day 1: IT and workspace setup, ID card issuance, HR induction
Day 2: Department orientation, mentor assignment
Day 3-5: Mandatory compliance trainings (InfoSec, Code of Conduct, POSH)
Day 30: Confirmation review with manager
